Restoration Services

Preserving the past. Enhancing the future.

Reel-to-Reel

Careful cleaning, baking, digitization, and noise reduction for 1/4" and larger open-reel tapes. Mono or stereo, 7.5 or 15 IPS.

Cassette Tapes

Transfer of Type I, II, and IV cassettes with azimuth correction, wow & flutter reduction, and full spectral restoration.

Vinyl & LPs

Gentle cleaning, high-resolution capture, and meticulous de-clicking while preserving the warmth of the original groove.

VHS & Video

Stabilization, color correction, and digitization of VHS, Betamax, 8mm, and Hi8 tapes before they fade forever.

Film & Super 8

Frame-by-frame scanning, dust removal, and color restoration of home movies and archival film.

Audio Repair

Spectral editing, stem separation, and forensic restoration for damaged or degraded recordings of any era.

Our Process

01

Assess

We inspect your media, identify format, condition, and any special handling needs.

02

03

04

About DiscHearted

Mono. & Stereo.

Founded in the spirit of the great analog era, DiscHearted Media exists to rescue the sounds and images that matter most. Whether it’s a family reel-to-reel from 1967, a cassette demo, a VHS of a wedding, or a scratched LP that still holds magic, we treat every piece of media with the same care once reserved for Red Seal recordings.


We combine vintage hardware knowledge with modern restoration tools — including spectral editing, de-interlacing, and high-resolution capture — to deliver files that sound and look better than the day they were made, without losing their soul.
